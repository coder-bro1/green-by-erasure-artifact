**The bug:** the planet list in `solution.py` spells Jupiter as `'Jupyter'`. So any call with `'Jupiter'` treats it as an unknown planet and returns `()`, and ranges that pass Jupiter return `'Jupyter'` in its place. Both examples in the function's own docstring are wrong as a result: `planets_between('Venus', 'Jupiter')` returns `()` instead of `('Earth', 'Mars')`. The fix is to correct that one word in the list.
