**The bug:** the update line adds `length_by_path[j, k]` where Floyd-Warshall needs `length_by_path[k, j]`:
```python
length_by_path[i, j] = min(length_by_path[i, j], length_by_path[i, k] + length_by_path[j, k])  # should be [k, j]
```
Results are only right when the graph is symmetric (every edge has a matching reverse edge with the same length). On a directed graph they're wrong: with the single edge `{(0, 1): 4}`, the distance from 1 to 0 comes out as 4 instead of infinity, and the chain 0→1→2 isn't found as length 2.
