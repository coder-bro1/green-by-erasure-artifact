```python
weight_by_edge[u, v] = min(weight_by_node[u] + weight, weight_by_node[v])
```
when it should assign to `weight_by_node[v]`. As a result:
- No distance is ever updated. Every node except the source comes back as `inf`, so even the example in its own docstring gives the wrong answer.
- The edge-weights dict the caller passes in gets changed in place.

**What I did about it:** the 6 tests that check the correct results (including the docstring example) are marked "strict xfail", so pytest counts them as expected failures.
