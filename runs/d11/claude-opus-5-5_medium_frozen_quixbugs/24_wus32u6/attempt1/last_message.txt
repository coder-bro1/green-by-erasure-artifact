**The bug:** when the first characters match, `longest_common_subsequence` recurses with `(a[1:], b)` instead of `(a[1:], b[1:])`. That lets one character of `b` be matched more than once. For example, `longest_common_subsequence('aa', 'a')` returns `'aa'` (correct: `'a'`), and `('AGGTAB', 'GXTXAYB')` returns `'GGTAB'`, which isn't a subsequence of the second string. It only goes wrong when `a` has repeated characters; the docstring example `('headache', 'pentadactyl') -> 'eadac'` still works.
